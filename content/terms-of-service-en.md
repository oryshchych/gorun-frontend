## 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 1.1

This Agreement is a public offer in accordance with Article 633 of the Civil Code of Ukraine and comes into force from the moment it is published on the website  
**https://gorun.team** (hereinafter – the **Website**).

### 1.2

Acceptance (full and unconditional acceptance) of the terms of the Agreement is carried out by:

- registering for the Event via the Website and/or
- paying the registration fee (slot) for participation in the Event.

From the moment of acceptance, the Agreement is considered concluded between the Organizer and the Participant.

### 1.3 Definitions:

- **Event** – a sports running event (race, meeting, trail, etc.) organized by NGO “GORUN”.
- **Registration fee** – payment for participation in the Event (slot, starter pack, timing, etc.).
- **Participant** – an individual who has accepted the offer.

---

## 2. SUBJECT OF THE AGREEMENT

### 2.1

The Organizer undertakes to provide the Participant with services for organizing participation in the selected Event, including:

- registration;
- starter pack;
- route organization;
- medical assistance (within reasonable limits);
- finisher medal, etc.

### 2.2

The Participant undertakes to:

- pay the registration fee;
- comply with the Event rules.

---

## 3. REGISTRATION AND PAYMENT PROCEDURE

### 3.1

Registration is carried out through the Website **https://gorun.team** and includes:

- filling out the registration form;
- selecting a distance;
- payment via **Plata by mono** (or other available methods).

### 3.2

The cost of the registration fee is indicated on the page of the relevant Event on the Website and may change.

### 3.3

Payment is made online. After payment, the Participant receives confirmation to the specified email address.

### 3.4

The registration fee is not a charitable donation but payment for services.

---

## 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES

### 4.1 The Organizer has the right to:

- change Event conditions (route, timing, etc.) in case of force majeure circumstances (weather, government restrictions, etc.);
- cancel the Event and refund the registration fee (minus actual expenses).

### 4.2 The Participant has the right to:

- receive services according to the paid slot;
- transfer the slot to another person according to the rules specified on the Website (if allowed for the specific Event).

### 4.3 The Participant undertakes to:

- be of legal age or have parental/guardian consent for minors;
- have an appropriate health condition (the Organizer is not responsible for the Participant’s health);
- comply with safety and ethical behavior rules.

---

## 5. REFUNDS

### 5.1 In case of Participant withdrawal:

- **14 days before the Event date** – 100% of the registration fee is refunded (minus payment system fees);
- **less than 14 days** – no refund is provided (the service is considered rendered).

### 5.2

In case of Event cancellation initiated by the Organizer, a full refund is provided.

### 5.3

Refunds are made to the bank card used for payment within **10 (ten) days** from the moment of request sent to:  
**Gorunteam.ua@gmail.com**
